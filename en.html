About

What is Obriy?

An unofficial, non-commercial fan project inspired by the Survarium universe

Reviving the spirit of Survarium

Obriy is an unofficial fan project inspired by the Survarium universe. The project is not affiliated with VG Entertainment Ltd. and is not an official sequel or expansion to Survarium.

We cannot bring Survarium back as it once was, but we aim to recreate its atmosphere and spirit. This is a non-commercial effort to revive familiar ideas, reimagine them, and bring them into a new reality, where survival, conflict, and exploration of lands lost to chaos once again become an essential part of life.

This project is for everyone who remembers Survarium and wants to experience its magic once more, even in a slightly different world. We hope to touch the hearts of everyone who was once part of that story.

📋 This project was created in accordance with the UGC Guidelines of VG Entertainment Ltd. Survarium™ is the property of VG Entertainment Ltd.

Development status

OBRIY is in active development and testing. The project is constantly evolving: gameplay mechanics are being improved, locations are being optimized, new features are being added, and reported bugs are being fixed.

Please note that the current version of the game is not the final release. Some elements may be unstable, subject to change, or still under development.

We welcome constructive feedback, comments, and suggestions, as they help us identify shortcomings and steadily improve OBRIY.

Thank you for your understanding, support, and involvement in the project's development!

Universe

OBRIY - The Story of the World

The world as we knew it is a thing of the past.

World War III changed the face of the planet forever. Ruined cities, radioactive contamination, anomalies, and mutations became the consequences of human decisions that threatened the very existence of civilization.

Where life once thrived, ruins and silence now reign. Nature is gradually reclaiming its lost territories, engulfing the remnants of the human world. Yet its rebirth conceals dangers humanity was not prepared to face.

Those who survived must adapt to a new reality. The struggle for territory, resources, and survival gives rise to new conflicts. Even after a global catastrophe, humanity has failed to find common ground in the face of its own extinction.

But is the world truly doomed to repeat the mistakes of the past?

OBRIY — the Ukrainian word for “horizon” — is more than the boundary between earth and sky. It is a symbol of the unknown future humanity is heading toward. A future shaped by the decisions it makes today.

For even after the darkest night, the sun will inevitably rise above the horizon. But what will its first rays reveal — the remnants of a shattered world, or the beginning of a new life?
